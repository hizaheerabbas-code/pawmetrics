Vet Bills Add Up Fast

The average dog owner in the USA spends $400–$700 per year on routine vet care alone. Emergency visits can easily add $1,000–$5,000 on top. Pet insurance can protect you from big unexpected bills.

🍖

Food Is the Biggest Cost

Food typically makes up 30–40% of total annual pet costs. Large breed dogs can cost $800–$1,500 per year in food alone depending on the quality of food you choose.

📈

Costs Rise With Age

Senior dogs and cats need more frequent vet visits, special diets and often medication. Budget for costs to increase by 20–40% once your pet reaches their senior years.

How Much Does a Dog or Cat Cost Per Year? Complete 2026 Guide

One of the most common questions from new pet owners is: how much does a pet really cost? The answer depends on many factors — breed size, where you live, the quality of food and whether you have pet insurance.

Average Annual Dog Cost in the USA

According to the American Pet Products Association, the average dog owner in the USA spends $1,500 to $3,000 per year on their dog. Large breed owners can spend significantly more — up to $4,000 or more when accounting for food, vet bills, grooming and insurance.

Average Annual Dog Cost in the UK

In the UK, the average annual cost of owning a dog is between £1,000 and £2,500 per year. The PDSA estimates that the lifetime cost of owning a medium-sized dog is over £16,000 from puppyhood to old age.

Average Annual Cat Cost

Cats are generally less expensive than dogs. In the USA, the average cat owner spends $800 to $1,500 per year. In the UK, annual costs typically range from £700 to £1,200. Cats do not need professional grooming or daily walks, which reduces costs significantly.

The Hidden Costs Most People Forget

Many new pet owners underestimate costs like pet sitting or boarding ($25–$85 per day), dental cleaning ($300–$700 per year), flea and tick prevention ($100–$200 per year), training classes ($100–$300) and toys and accessories ($100–$300 per year). Our calculator includes all of these so you get a realistic total.

Is Pet Insurance Worth It?

Pet insurance costs $30–$80 per month for dogs and $15–$40 per month for cats in the USA and UK. A single emergency vet visit can cost $1,000–$5,000. For most pet owners, insurance provides valuable peace of mind and can save thousands in an emergency.
